About

The team behind the paperwork.

We bring 8+ years of Canadian case-management documentation experience to a focused back-office practice — supporting individuals and agencies with organised, review-ready files.

Why Back Office

Back Office Solutions exists to own that documentation layer: checklists, organisation, quality control, and white-label delivery — without claiming government affiliation or visa outcomes.

What we stand for

  • Documentation and administrative case-management support only
  • Brand discretion for agency partners
  • Clear scope before work begins
  • Quality that shows in the file, not in marketing noise
